# Final replies

Read before the final reply of a substantial task, or before showing numbers. A quick answer or a
PR link needs only a few lines.

- **The result first, in plain words**: one or two sentences on what changed for the user. They
  often read on a phone and dictate their messages, so no jargon and no file lists.
- **Then something to see, when the tray looks or behaves differently**: a simulator GIF or MP4
  (before/after side by side when it's a change), embedded by absolute path. Show the real 16 px
  size; add a larger render only to make a detail visible, and say which is which.
- **Numbers that compare go in a chart, never a table**: sizes, timings, before/after. Use T3's
  `html_preview` to check it, then `html_render`. A table only for things that aren't numbers.
- Then short bullets:
  - what to try or decide;
  - what wasn't verified, said plainly;
  - the status: committed, pushed, the PR link, and what's installed on their PC, from which
    commit or tag.
- End with at most one question, or a one-line offer of the next step.
- Leave out unless asked: test counts, function names, how many checks you ran, "the docs are
  updated".
